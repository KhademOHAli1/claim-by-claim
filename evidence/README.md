It preserves each original source URL, source type, publication or version date, pinpoint, dependency group, and limitations. Repository paths are relative to the site project, which is the intended repository root.

## What is citation-only

Every other source remains represented by metadata and its original citation. A citation-only record is not an archive capture. These records do not imply that the source is still available, unchanged, independently authenticated, or fully inspected beyond the stated limits.

No complete copyrighted scientific books or papers, private DNA or identity exhibits, certificates, vehicle exhibits, or private address/genetic materials are reproduced in this repository. The registry’s retention status describes the public repository, not everything in the internal research collection. No new downloads were made to assemble this publication copy.

## Integrity and meaning

The registry records SHA-256 digests for the five PDFs and six previews. A matching digest establishes that a file matches this recorded byte version. It does not authenticate a document’s issuer, prove authorship, establish a trusted timestamp or complete chain of custody, or show that every allegation in the document is true.

A congressional inquiry is not a finding of guilt. A financial disclosure records the filer’s declarations and must be read with amendments. Source limitations and dependency groups remain part of the evidence assessment even when a copy is hosted here.

Original source links remain the primary citation route. The repository copies provide versioned access to the retained publication material and should be read with the accompanying review and source limitations.
